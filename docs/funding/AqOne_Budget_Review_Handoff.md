## 2. Perspectives: findings and practical solutions

### Devil's Advocate: funding and commercial assumptions

- **The request does not yet represent a fully costed pilot.**
  Add the missing safety, sea-access, maintenance and confirmed administrative costs before setting the final ask.
  Retain a justified amount even if it exceeds PHP 1.5 million.
- **The investor cash model needs reconciliation with the Year 1 funding plan.**
  Identify whether its PHP 800,000 Year 1 fixed-cost line overlaps the grant budget or represents additional costs.
  Assuming all Year 1 costs are externally covered and Year 2 opens with PHP 3 million, the summary's figures leave only about PHP 320,000 before the second tranche, before working-capital and tax adjustments.
  Use monthly collections, inventory purchases and funding dates rather than year-end balances alone.
- **Commercial returns rely heavily on unverified hardware demand and cost reductions.**
  Pod sales account for 65.7% of base-case Year 5 revenue.
  Test flat production costs, slower adoption, pod-service churn, delayed payments and no exit.
  Any cash deficit must show its financing, dilution or reduced-expansion consequence; the existing flat-cost stress test leaves investor returns unchanged despite funding deficits.

### Simplicity Champion: spend on deliverables and show the basis

- **Estimates are presented with more certainty than their sources support.**
  Distinguish quoted prices, estimates, planning quantities and institutional approvals.
  For major purchases, record supplier, date, model, quantity, shipping, taxes and quotation validity.
- **Some optional equipment needs a deliverable-level justification.**
  Preserve the agreed PHP 70,000 device allocation, with project ownership and performance requirements documented.
  Review the PHP 42,300 buoy WiFi package against the current architecture, which requires a measured operational purpose for stationary-buoy phone WiFi.
  Confirm the PHP 30,000 printing rate and required parts, and explain the PHP 126,504 AI-tool allocation through actual research/development workloads.
- **Procurement quantities should follow validation.**
  Price the proposed 3 buoy nodes and 2 gateways as planning quantities, then confirm sites, direct-link coverage and relay needs before full deployment procurement.
  Check existing tools, consumables and spare pods before adding duplicate allowances.

### Security and Reliability Auditor: acceptance, safety and ownership

- **The SOS acceptance criteria are incomplete.**
  Define drill count, test locations, delivery and acknowledgement deadlines, outage measurement, maximum continuous outage and power/carrier failure tests.
  A 95% uptime threshold permits 108 hours of downtime over an illustrative 90-day period; it needs a justified operating context.
- **Safety and access are not yet fully funded.**
  Price life vests, appropriate insurance, boat access, boat fuel and deployment assistance for field crews of up to five.
  Name the funding source for each item; institutional backing does not establish that those resources are provided.
- **Grant rules and ownership require ASU confirmation.**
  Resolve contingency eligibility, student stipend treatment, faculty/indirect costs and classification of the PHP 192,450 assembled buoy allocation.
  Separate ASU/grant assets from later company purchases and establish commercial IP rights before investor commitments.
  Government-funded research IP generally vests in the performing research institution, subject to exceptions and agreements. [RA 10055](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2010/ra_10055_2010.html)

### Architecture and Operations: complete costs and maintainable deployment

- **The pod estimate needs a complete assembled cost.**
  Include the physical SOS button, LiFePO4-compatible battery/charging system, wiring, mounting, assembly, testing and delivered procurement costs.
  Measure endurance and recharge time before presenting them as demonstrated capabilities.
- **Maintenance must be explicit.**
  Cost repairs, replacement components, retrieval and maintenance travel, with the two spare pods counted once.
  In the commercial model, PHP 300 per pod-year would be consumed by a roughly 1.75% annual full-pod replacement rate at PHP 17,150, before labour or travel.
- **Commercial margin and royalty definitions need repair.**
  At PHP 19,900 price and PHP 17,150 component cost, pod margin is 13.8%; the full PHP 750 royalty leaves PHP 2,000 before omitted costs.
  Define deployment margin separately from infrastructure investment and recurring service costs.
  The stated Year 2 half-rate royalty gives PHP 75,000, while the table's PHP 56,250 matches 37.5% paid-in scaling; select one rule and reconcile the model.
  If weather-tiered check-ins enter the funded scope, cost their separate receiver and the current 80-pod-per-receiver capacity limit.

### Travel treatment

Use a transparent "Field travel and transportation" line showing the ASU Kalibo-to-site route, purpose, crew, trips and approved reimbursement basis.
Changing the label alone does not establish eligibility for private-car fuel reimbursement.
The DOST-hosted government travel rules exclude gasoline reimbursement for private vehicles and provide equivalent customary transportation costs instead. [EO 77, Section 5(a)](https://pcieerd.dost.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Travel_Guidelines_Local.pdf)
ASU Accounting must confirm the applicable treatment for student grant participants before retaining the existing PHP 1,250-per-trip rate.
Identify sea transport and boat fuel separately.
