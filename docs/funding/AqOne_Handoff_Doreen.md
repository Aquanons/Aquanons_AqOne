## 6. Say this, avoid that

Say:
- "We are asking DOST to fund a measured SOS pilot in New Washington; AI features will be evaluated experimentally alongside it."
- "Every unit cost comes from our itemized budget; lines marked estimate are being quoted."
- "Investors commit now at today's valuation but pay only after each milestone is verified."
- "The DOST grant de-risks the technology; private capital scales it after the pilot."

Avoid:
- Claiming the shoreline is already covered. There is no measured field coverage yet.
- Saying the LGU has agreed to anything. A letter was sent; no reply yet. Boats, buoys, gateway sites and permits are dependencies.
- Presenting Heltec range figures as pod performance. The pods use RAK boards and their endurance is not yet measured.
- "National waters". Say "municipal waters" (the 15 km legal boundary).
- Treating the investor numbers as forecasts. They are scenarios; prices, adoption and exit multiples are assumptions.

## 7. Questions judges or investors will ask, and honest answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Why ₱1.7M and not ₱1.5M? | Safety gear, sea access, maintenance spares and the pod SOS button were missing; the pilot is not dependable without them. |
| Who owns the IP? | To be settled with ASU before any investment (government-funded research IP generally belongs to the institution under RA 10055). |
| What if pods don't sell? | Pods are 65.7% of base Y5 revenue and the biggest risk. With half the pod adoption, base-case returns fall to 1.99× and the company needs ₱0.53M of bridge money. |
| Why so much equity value? | The ₱50M and ₱75M valuations are negotiable inputs; royalties return most of the money first. |
| What do investors get if there's no exit? | Royalties only: 0.53× by Y5 in the base case, continuing until ₱8M is repaid. |
| Margins on pods? | ₱2,400 per pod (12.1%) at ₱19,900; ₱1,650 after the ₱750 royalty. Cheaper custom boards are the target. |

Stress tests (base case, one change at a time):

| Test | Bridge needed | Money multiple | IRR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| As built | ₱0 | 3.15× | 42% |
| Half pod adoption | ₱0.53M | 1.99× | 23% |
| Pod cost never falls | ₱7.92M | 2.90× | 38% |
| LGU fee ₱250k | ₱0.16M | 2.97× | 39% |
| Cancellations 20%/yr | ₱0 | 2.93× | 39% |
| Fee ₱250k + half pods | ₱6.11M | 1.71× | 18% |

Bridge money is assumed at the ₱75M valuation, which is generous; a real distressed bridge would dilute more.
